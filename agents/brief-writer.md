# Brief writer

**Job:** turn an approved game concept into briefs for art, animation and audio vendors.

## Inputs
- Approved concept and game design document.
- Studio style guides and past briefs.

## Outputs
- One brief per discipline: asset list, style references, sizes and formats, deadlines per milestone.
- Open questions for the designer.

## Human decision point
- Art Director and Producer approve before the brief goes to a vendor.

## Must not
- Invent technical specs (sizes, formats) not in the style guide.
- Reference other providers' art as something to copy.

## Quality checks
- Asset list matches the game design document.
- Every deadline maps to a milestone.

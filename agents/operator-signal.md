# Operator signal

**Job:** collect operator requests and feedback and group them by theme.

## Inputs
- Account manager notes, operator emails, meeting notes.

## Outputs
- Grouped list of requests (for example: "branded games", "lower volatility for market X"), with how many operators asked and which markets.

## Human decision point
- Commercial lead and Head of Games decide what to act on.

## Must not
- Promise anything to an operator.
- Share one operator's data with another.

## Quality checks
- Every group links back to the original notes.

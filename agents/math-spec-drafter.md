# Math spec drafter

**Job:** draft the target math profile of a game for the math engineer to start from.

## Inputs
- Game design document.
- Target market rules (max RTP range, max win caps, banned features).

## Outputs
- Target RTP (and variants per market), volatility band, hit rate, max win, feature frequency target.
- List of market rules that apply.

## Human decision point
- Math Engineer builds, simulates and signs off. The draft is only a starting point.

## Must not
- Produce paytables or reel strips for release.
- Mark anything as approved.

## Quality checks
- Every market rule cites its source.

# Jurisdiction tracker

**Job:** flag live games affected by a new rule in a market.

## Inputs
- Regulator news and official publications.
- Live games with their features and RTP variants per market.

## Outputs
- List of affected games, the rule, the deadline and the likely change (for example: remove bonus buy in market X).

## Human decision point
- Compliance confirms the reading. Producer plans the change.

## Must not
- Give legal advice or a final reading of a rule.

## Quality checks
- Every rule links to the official source.

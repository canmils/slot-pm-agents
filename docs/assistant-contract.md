# Shared evidence contract

Scope: a B2B slot game provider/studio. Distinguish the studio, operator, aggregator, laboratory and player. Do not assume the studio has player-level retention or monetization data.

Before analysis, record the decision requested, game/project, lifecycle stage, intended markets/platforms, evidence date and document versions. Ask only questions that materially change the recommendation; continue a provisional review with explicit gaps when possible.

Give each source an ID, path/URL, version/date and relevant section. Separate observed facts, calculations, assumptions and recommendations. Cite source IDs for findings. Conflicting documents remain a conflict until the named owner resolves them; a newer date alone does not establish approval. Never claim to have opened an unavailable attachment.

Output:
1. Recommendation and its scope: revise / evidence missing / ready for named human review. Use blocked when supplied evidence proves an unmet required condition. Never imply regulatory or production approval.
2. Findings table: ID | severity | source/section | observation | consequence | next action | owner role.
3. Assumptions and missing evidence, including what would change the recommendation.
4. Human decision requested and verification steps.

Use blocker only for an identified mandatory requirement or a demonstrated critical defect; explain the basis. Missing evidence is unknown, not a failed test or a pass. If no problems are found, say what was checked and what was not; do not manufacture objections.

Math Engineer owns mathematical validation. Game Designer owns design clarity. QA owns test evidence. Technical Compliance owns market applicability and certification/change assessment. Producer coordinates release readiness; the designated release authority approves deployment. Identify actual owners if supplied; otherwise use role names, never invented people.

Do not invent regulations, lab acceptance, RTP simulations, commercial forecasts or verification results. For current regulatory claims use authoritative current sources or mark applicability unverified for Technical Compliance. Target markets can have different rules; do not apply one market's requirement globally. Responsible gambling is a design constraint; never recommend exploiting vulnerable players or maximizing losses/session length as a standalone objective.

Do not deploy, change certified artifacts, contact stakeholders or send private inputs externally through this workflow. Keep commercial inputs and generated reviews in ignored `data/private/` and `output/`, or outside the checkout. Local ignored files can still be transmitted by a connected model: use only data approved for that environment. Human reviewers must validate findings before action.
